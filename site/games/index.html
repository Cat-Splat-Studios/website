From specification to implementation.

A Cat Splat Studios-led Unreal project, developed to our gameplay and technical specifications. The programming spans player control, enemy behaviour and encounter systems.

Behaviour-tree AI and a combat director support encounters that respond to the action. Custom movement systems connect how the craft handles with how enemies engage it.

Showcase · Unity

NEON KEYS

Neon maze game · Desktop browser

Levels by a local AI agent.

A short game you can play in a desktop browser. A local AI agent on one gaming PC, with no cloud service, designed its five levels, and every level was proven finishable. Frontier AI built most of the game around them.

Its HOW IT WAS MADE screen shows the setup and the numbers.
